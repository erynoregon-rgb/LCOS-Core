## SU3 Opcode Isomorphism (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20499960, dated 2026-06-01)
**Central claim refuted by the authors' own verification apparatus, July 2026.**
The verification script compared permuted generators against structure constants
derived from the identity ordering — a comparison-oracle defect under which only
the identity permutation could ever pass. The claimed unique isomorphism is a
tautology. Surviving content is trivial (3-bit cardinality; standard Gell-Mann
identities).

The artifact is retained and repointed rather than deleted, per this project's
research-integrity doctrine (corrections are typed and preserved, never silently
removed): the full document with its refutation banner remains in place as a case
study in an internal claim-verification process catching its author's own
overclaim. A v2 erratum will be posted to the Zenodo record so the concept DOI
resolves to the correction.

Timeline: claim published 2026-06-01; refuted internally 2026-07 (oracle defect
identified, retraction record SU3_OPCODE_ISOMORPHISM_RECEIPT_A1); publicly
repointed 2026-08-27.
